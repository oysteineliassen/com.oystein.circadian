Circadian Lighting adjusts the colour temperature and brightness of your lights through the day: warm and dim in the evening and at night, cool and bright in the daytime.

Put your lights into rooms. Each room follows sunrise and sunset, or your own points on a 24-hour clock. Rooms can have their own plan for weekends and public holidays, a sunrise wake-up, and brightness that adapts to daylight from a light sensor.

Lights that are switched off are left alone, and a light you adjust yourself is left alone until you resume it. When a light is switched on, it gets the right colour straight away.

The settings page shows every room and light at a glance, a 24-hour clock to edit the plan, and a history graph. Flow cards let you pause rooms, start a wake-up, and react when a light is adjusted by hand.
